Technical overview

A database for knowledge that evolves.

AtoposDB is being built as a native temporal graph database for AI memory engineering. This overview explains the intended model and workflows, with development directions distinguished from public availability.

Connect knowledge with its context and history.

The intended model connects recorded knowledge, decisions, events, and evidence. Historical views help distinguish what was recorded at the time from later corrections. Individual and project scopes make knowledge sharing explicit.

AtoposDB is the database foundation. Agents and models interpret information and produce predictions; the database is being designed to preserve the context, provenance, and results needed to inspect that work.

People and agents can build knowledge, carry it across sessions and providers, share it with a project, and inspect how later evidence changes earlier conclusions.

We’re developing AtoposDB as an engine, integrated components, and a complete collaborative workspace.

Continue the work. Keep the context.

Connect recorded knowledge to the work that produced it, so people and agents can pick it up across sessions, channels, and providers. Preserve the evidence, decisions, and corrections that turn experience into reusable expertise.

Useful continuity includes the source, scope, and history of a record. Retrieving a related memory should also make its supporting evidence and later corrections available for inspection.

Shared memory for AI agents.

Give agents access to the project knowledge they need. Share context, decisions, and evidence while keeping individual and project memory distinct.

Connect knowledge, decisions, and evidence so agents can work from a common understanding.

Reconstruct the context behind past decisions.

Trace the recorded knowledge, decisions, and evidence behind earlier work. Reconstruct what was known at the time, then examine how later evidence changed the conclusions.

Give your AI agents the situational awareness they need.

Connect agents to the project context, past decisions, and evidence that help them understand what has changed—and decide what to do next.

Learn from the shape of information.

We’re building AtoposDB to support graph embeddings and representation learning over connected knowledge and its history. Compare patterns in relationships and events to find relevant past experience, then inspect the underlying context and evidence to decide what applies.

Build representations of graphs, subgraphs, and histories to explore structural similarity, retrieve related cases, and develop models that learn from connected information.

Help your agents explore “what if?”

Agents can use recorded experience to explore possible futures and compare choices.

We’re building AtoposDB as a foundation for AI thought experiments, scenario planning, and predictive modeling. Connect alternative scenarios to their assumptions, historical context, evidence, and model-generated outcomes. Compare possibilities while keeping simulated outcomes distinct from observed results, then revisit predictions as new evidence arrives.

Preserve scenario inputs, model versions, and results so experiments can be inspected, compared, and evaluated against real outcomes.
